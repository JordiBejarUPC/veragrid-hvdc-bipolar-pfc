# Commit message policy

Every commit must have a concise subject followed by a non-empty body. The body must use blank-separated paragraphs that explain:

- `Why:` the problem or motivation.
- `Scope:` or `What:` the exact change.
- `Validation:` the architectural or manual checks performed.
- `Tests:` the commands and results, or `None known` when no test evidence exists.
- `Risks:` the remaining risk, or `None known` when no specific risk is known.

Do not invent validation or test evidence. Preserve this format when amending, rebasing, cherry-picking, reverting, or merging commits.
